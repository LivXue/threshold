Read its moves

A molten core burns inside a machine taller than the plant's columns. Five deadly attacks stand between you and that core. Read its moves. Find your opening.

  1. Ground slam

    Stay clear of the impact and jump over the spreading shockwave.

  2. Core beam

    The beam locks onto your position. Dodge sideways to escape.

  3. Slag barrage

    Molten slag rains down from over thirty meters above. Watch where it will land and get clear.

  4. Charge

    It hurls its entire bulk straight at you. Get out of its path.

  5. Tail sweep

    Keep your distance and jump over the shockwave sweeping across the floor.

First prototype

The arena rose above a furnace pit. The Warden took its current form. The stage was set for the final showdown.
